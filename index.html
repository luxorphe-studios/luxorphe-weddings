Wedding photography & film

Your day, exactly as it felt.

We photograph weddings the way they really happen. The two of us work quietly beside you and catch what no one could stage: a hand squeezed before the aisle, a father’s laugh, the dance floor at midnight. Then, for a few unhurried minutes, we step in and make portraits with the polish of a fashion editorial.

We were born in Africa, shaped by Britain and Canada, and now call Toronto and Montréal home. That mix shows in our work: classic and warm, never stiff, and always made with love.

Our approach

Two ways of seeing

I — DOCUMENTARY

Nothing staged. Everything felt.

We capture the natural flow of your day: the laughter, the nerves, the glances across the room. We stay close, quietly, so you can live every minute of it.

II — EDITORIAL

Portraits with an editor’s eye.

Laurette’s eye for styling, posing and composition meets Yissegnon’s light. The result is images and films that feel both timeless and refined.

III — TORONTO & MONTRÉAL

Two cities we know by heart.

With over six years photographing weddings here, we know the venues, the light and the timings. Our insider knowledge comes with a luxury service from start to finish.

Your photographers

Laurette & Yissegnon

Yissegnon Avosse

Lead photographer, editor, videographer

African-born Yissegnon has lived in Montréal and Toronto for decades. He knows the cities’ most popular wedding venues after six years as a professional wedding photographer, and he is an expert retoucher. He dedicates countless hours to capturing the beauty and true essence of Black skin.

Fellow, SWPPMaster, WPPI

Laurette Metogo

Assistant photographer, make-up artist, stylist

Laurette is a creative make-up artist and assistant photographer with a strong sense of styling and posing. Her work leans towards pictorial composition, shaped by more than five years in the industry. Born in Africa, she is now a Canadian citizen and a sought-after artist.
